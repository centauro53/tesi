\begin{exercise}

	Let $R$ pp formula and let $M,N$ ec models of $\mathcal{T}^{\pm}$. Suppose that $R$ has the same number of solutions in $M,N$.

\end{exercise}

\begin{proof}

	Suppose $M$ has exactly $k$ solutions. 
	We can express "there are at most $k$ solutions" as:
	\[
		\forall x_1,..,x_k,x_{k+1} (\bigwedge_{i = 1,..,k+1} R(x_i)  \to \bigvee_{i < j} x_i = x_j)
	\]

	This is preserved by 2) of the lemma, as "=" will be expressible by a pp-type.

	We can express the negation of "there are at least $k$ solutions" as:
	\[
		\forall x_1,..,x_k  (\bigwedge_{i=1,..,k} R(x_i) \to \bigvee_{i < j} x_i = x_j)
	\]

	This is preserved by 2) of the lemma as "=" will be expressible by a pp-type.

\end{proof}
