\begin{exercise}

	The class of e.c. models of $\mathcal{T}$ admits amalgamation.

\end{exercise}

\begin{proof}

	Let $f:A \to B$ and $g: A \to C$ maps between e.c. models of $\mathcal{T}$.
	We want to find $D \models \mathcal{T}$ (not necessarily e.c. yet) such that there exist $h: B \to D$ and $l: C \to D$ witnessing amalgamation.
	We just need to prove that the following type is consistent: 
	\[
		\mathcal{T} \cup at-tp(B) \cup at-tp(C)
	\]

	where elements of the form $f(a),g(a)$ are nominated as $a$.
	This can be done because (since $A,B,C$ e.c.) $f,g,h,l$ embeddings so we can identify an element with its image.
	Then taking $h(f(a)) = a^D = l(g(a))$ we will have amalgamation on $D$.
	Then consider the direct limit construction to have $i: D \to E$.
	This gives that $i \circ h$ and $i \circ l$ are the desired maps.
	Now, by contradiction assume the above type is inconsistent.
	Then it is finitely inconsistent. Let $\psi_1(b,a),..,\psi_n(b,a) \in at-tp(B)$ and $\theta(c,a),..,\theta(c,a) \in at-tp(C)$ with $b,c$ tuple of constants
	different from those nominated by $A$,
	such that 
	\[
		\mathcal{T} \cup \{\bigwedge_{i=1,..,n} \psi_i(b,a)\} \cup \{\bigwedge_{j=1,..,m} \theta_j(c,a) \}
	\]
	inconsistent.
	This is the same as 
	\[
		\mathcal{T} \cup \{ \exists y \bigwedge_{i=1,..,n} \psi_i(y,a) \} \cup \{ \exists z \bigwedge_{j=1,..,m} \theta_j(z,a) \}
	\]
	inconsistent.
	But by hypothesis $A$ is e.c. model of $\mathcal{T}$ and, 
	as $B \models \exists y \bigwedge_{i=1,..,n} \psi(y,f(a))$ and $C \models \exists z \bigwedge_{j=1,..,m} \theta_j(z,g(a))$,
	we have that $A$ is a model of the above type, contradiction.

\end{proof}
